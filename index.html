Better selfies with the better camera.

The back camera takes far better photos than the front one, but you can't see the screen. SelfieBuddy watches for you: it tells you out loud how to aim the phone, waits until the light, focus and your smile look right, then takes the photo by itself.

How it works

Prop up your phone or hold it out, with the back camera facing you.

Tap Start Selfie

Point the back camera toward yourself and step into the frame.

Follow the voice

“Tip the phone up.” “Turn the phone to your left.” “Give us a smile.” One clear tip at a time.

Hold still

When everything looks right, the beeps stop. A second later you'll hear the shutter, and the photo goes straight to your library.

A coach in your pocket

Hear what to do, without needing to see the screen.

🗣️

Voice Guidance

Spoken directions to tip the phone up or down, turn it left or right, and move closer or step back.

🔔

Sound Cues

Parking-sensor beeps that speed up as you get closer, with a different sound for turning, tipping up or down, and moving closer. Works with the silent switch on.

✨

Smart Auto-Capture

Takes the photo for you after you've held a good pose for a second. No timer, no running back and forth.

🔄

Portrait or Landscape

A full-screen camera view in either orientation, with photos always saved upright.
