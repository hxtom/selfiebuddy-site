Getting Started

How do I take my first selfie?
  1. Prop your phone up or hold it out with the back camera facing you.
  2. Tap the Start button. It shows your guidance mode, for example Start Voice & Beep Guide.
  3. Follow the voice tips and beeps, or the tip on screen.
  4. When you're in position, hold still. About a second later the photo is taken with a shutter sound and saved to your Photos library.

Each Start takes one photo. You can also tap the round shutter button at any time to take a photo yourself.

Why does SelfieBuddy use the back camera?

The back camera has a better sensor and lens than the front camera, so your selfies come out sharper and cleaner. SelfieBuddy's voice and sound cues stand in for the screen you can't see.

Is there a guide inside the app?

Yes. Tap the gear button on the camera screen, then How to Use.

Does it work in landscape?

Yes. Turn your phone sideways and the camera view and controls rearrange to fill the screen. Photos are always saved the right way up.

Guidance Modes

What are the guidance modes?

Use the speaker button (beeps) and the voice button at the bottom left of the camera screen, or Settings → Guidance:

  • Voice & Beep Guide (both on): spoken tips plus beeps. Beeps pause while the voice is talking.
  • Voice Guide (voice only): spoken tips only.
  • Beep Guide (beeps only): beeps only, no talking.
  • Auto Pilot (both off): no beeps or voice. Tap Start Auto Pilot and SelfieBuddy quietly takes one photo, with a shutter sound, as soon as you're in position.

The current mode is shown on the Start button and under the SelfieBuddy title.

Beeps and Voice

What do the different beeps mean?
  • One beep a second: looking for you. Point the camera toward yourself.
  • Beeps speeding up: you're getting closer to a good position, like a parking sensor.
  • Plain beeps: turn the phone left or right. With headphones, they come from the side to turn toward.
  • Rising “bwoop”: tip the phone up.
  • Falling “bwoop”: tip the phone down.
  • Double “bip-bip”: move closer or take a step back.
  • Very fast beeps: you're in position; it's waiting for the light, focus, eyes or smile check.
  • Silence: everything's right. Hold still.
  • Click-clack: photo taken.
When it says “turn the phone to your left”, whose left is that?

Yours, as you stand facing the back camera. If a friend is holding the phone from behind, left and right are reversed for them.

Can I change the voice?

Yes. Open Settings in SelfieBuddy (the gear button), then Voice, and tap a voice to hear a sample. Automatic picks the best voice installed on your iPhone. For the most natural sound, download a Premium or Enhanced voice in the iPhone's Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English; it then appears in the list.

I can't hear anything.
  • Check that the speaker (beeps) or voice button is on. With both off, SelfieBuddy is in Auto Pilot and only plays the shutter sound.
  • Sounds only play after you tap Start, and stop once your photo is taken or when you tap Stop.
  • Turn up the volume. SelfieBuddy plays its cues even with the silent switch on. With Bluetooth headphones connected, the sound plays through them.

Auto-Capture and Quality Checks

Why isn't it taking the photo automatically?
  • Make sure Auto is switched on and that you've tapped Start.
  • Look at the tip on screen. It shows what still needs fixing.
  • Check the Wait for chips (Light, Focus, Eyes, Smile). Auto-capture waits until every check that's switched on passes. Tap a chip to turn it off.
  • Hold the pose for about a second once you're in position.
I don't want to smile in every photo.

Tap the Smile chip to turn that check off. Your choices are remembered.

Why are Eyes and Smile greyed out?

At .5× (ultra-wide) zoom your face is too small to read reliably, so those two checks pause. They come back when you zoom to 1× or more.

The Light check won't pass.

Face toward a window or lamp so the light falls on your face, and avoid standing with a bright light directly behind you. You can also tap the light button to turn on the flashlight.

Zoom and Photo Shape

How do I zoom?

Tap .5, 1×, 2 or your phone's telephoto zoom (for example 5) above the shutter button. The buttons available depend on your iPhone's cameras. Your choice is remembered.

What do 4:3 and Full mean?

Tap the shape button next to the zoom buttons. 4:3 keeps the camera's whole image. Full matches your iPhone's screen and is cropped from the centre. The area outside your photo is dimmed, and the framing tips use the shape you chose. Your choice is remembered.

Saving Photos

Where do my photos go?

Every photo is saved straight to your Photos library in full quality, keeping its date and location. The thumbnail in the corner of the camera screen shows your latest one.

My photos aren't being saved.

SelfieBuddy needs permission to add photos. Go to Settings → Apps → SelfieBuddy → Photos and choose Add Photos Only or Full Access.

The camera says “Camera paused”.

Another app is using the camera, SelfieBuddy is sharing the screen with another app, or your iPhone is too warm. The camera resumes by itself once that's resolved.

The camera screen is black.

Go to Settings → Apps → SelfieBuddy and turn on Camera. Also make sure no other app is using the camera.

SelfieBuddy Pro

What's free, and what does Pro unlock?

The free version includes every feature for your first 50 photos. The badge at the top of the camera screen shows how many free photos you have left. Pro is a one-time purchase that removes the limit. There is no subscription.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account you bought Pro with. In SelfieBuddy, tap the gear button for Settings (or the badge at the top of the camera screen), then tap Restore Purchases.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

Is the camera view recorded or uploaded?

No. The live camera view is analyzed on your device and immediately discarded. Only the photos you take are saved, and only to your own library. See the Privacy Policy for details.
