Getting Started

How do I take my first selfie?
  1. Prop your phone up or hold it out with the back camera facing you.
  2. Tap Start Selfie.
  3. Follow the spoken tips until you hear a steady tone, then hold still. With Auto on, SelfieBuddy takes the photo for you.

You can also tap the round shutter button at any time to take a photo yourself.

Why does SelfieBuddy use the back camera?

The back camera has a better sensor and lens than the front camera, so your selfies come out sharper and cleaner. SelfieBuddy's voice and sound cues stand in for the screen you can't see.

Does it work in landscape?

Yes. Turn your phone sideways and the camera view and controls rearrange to fill the screen. Photos are always saved the right way up.

Voice and Sounds

When it says “turn the phone to your left”, whose left is that?

Yours, as you stand facing the back camera. If a friend is holding the phone from behind, left and right are reversed for them.

What do the different beeps mean?
  • Slow beeps: looking for you. Point the camera toward yourself.
  • Quick, low beeps: almost there. Follow the tip on screen or the voice.
  • Steady tone: everything looks good. Hold still.
  • Click-clack: photo taken.
How do I turn off the voice or the sounds?

Tap the voice button (person with sound waves) to turn spoken tips on or off while keeping the beeps. Tap the speaker button to mute everything. Sounds only play after you tap Start Selfie, and stop once your photo is taken or when you tap Stop.

I can't hear anything.

Check that the speaker button isn't muted and turn up your volume. SelfieBuddy plays its cues even with the silent switch on. If you're using Bluetooth headphones, the sound will play through them.

Auto-Capture and Quality Checks

Why isn't it taking the photo automatically?
  • Make sure Auto is switched on and that you've tapped Start Selfie.
  • Look at the tip on screen. It shows what still needs fixing.
  • Check the Wait for chips (Light, Focus, Eyes, Smile). Auto-capture waits until every check that's switched on passes. Tap a chip to turn it off.
  • Hold the pose for about a second once you hear the steady tone.
I don't want to smile in every photo.

Tap the Smile chip to turn that check off. Your choices are remembered.

The Light check won't pass.

Face toward a window or lamp so the light falls on your face, and avoid standing with a bright light directly behind you.

Saving Photos

Where do my photos go?

Every photo is saved straight to your Photos library in full quality. The thumbnail in the corner of the camera screen shows your latest one.

My photos aren't being saved.

SelfieBuddy needs permission to add photos. Go to Settings → Apps → SelfieBuddy → Photos and choose Add Photos Only or Full Access.

The camera screen is black.

Go to Settings → Apps → SelfieBuddy and turn on Camera. Also make sure no other app is using the camera.

SelfieBuddy Pro

What's free, and what does Pro unlock?

The free version includes every feature for your first 50 photos. The badge at the top of the camera screen shows how many free photos you have left. Pro is a one-time purchase that removes the limit. There is no subscription.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account you bought Pro with. In SelfieBuddy, tap the badge at the top of the camera screen, then tap Restore Purchases.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

Is the camera view recorded or uploaded?

No. The live camera view is analyzed on your device and immediately discarded. Only the photos you take are saved, and only to your own library. See the Privacy Policy for details.
