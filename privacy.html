Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 21, 2026

SelfieBuddy is designed to work entirely on your device. It does not collect, sell or share personal data. The camera view is analyzed on your iPhone or iPad and is never recorded or uploaded.

1. What SelfieBuddy Accesses

  • Camera, only with your permission. SelfieBuddy uses the back camera to show the preview and take photos. While the camera is open, it analyzes the live view on your device to find you in the frame, check light and focus, and detect open eyes and a smile. These frames are processed in memory and discarded immediately; they are never saved, recorded or sent anywhere.
  • Photo library (add only), only with your permission. SelfieBuddy saves the photos you take to your library. It asks only for permission to add photos, so it cannot see, read or change the photos already in your library. You can change this at any time in Settings → Apps → SelfieBuddy → Photos.

SelfieBuddy does not use face recognition to identify who you are. It only detects whether a face is present and its expression, and none of this information is stored.

2. What Stays on Your Device

SelfieBuddy stores the following only on your device:

  • Your settings, such as which quality checks are on and whether voice tips are enabled.
  • The number of free photos you've taken.

Voice tips are generated by your device's built-in speech, not an online service.

3. What Leaves Your Device

  • Your photos are saved to your Photos library. If iCloud Photos is turned on, Apple syncs them to your other devices under the Apple Privacy Policy. SelfieBuddy itself never uploads them.
  • Purchases: SelfieBuddy Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; SelfieBuddy only receives confirmation that the purchase is valid.

4. What SelfieBuddy Does Not Do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No recording or uploading of the camera view.
  • No sharing or selling of data.

5. Children

SelfieBuddy does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
